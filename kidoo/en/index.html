p. 8

Questions

How does the other parent join?

In the Children tab, "Invite the other parent" shows a code and a QR code. The other parent installs Kidoo, chooses "Join a family" and scans it.

I'm on my own. Is Kidoo still useful?

Yes. A single parent can use every feature; the conversation and requests to the other parent appear once a second parent joins.

What if the other parent has an Android phone?

The Android app is in the works. It will join the same family as the iPhone, with the same schedule.

What happens after the free month?

Choose $14.99 a year or $3.99 a month (or your local price). Without a subscription, everything stays visible in read-only mode: nothing is deleted.

Who sees our data?

Only your family. Kidoo has no advertising or analytics tools.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings › Account › Delete my account, right in the app.
